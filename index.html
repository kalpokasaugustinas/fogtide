How to play

  • The map is split into segments. Each one you own grows 1% of its cap per second up to the cap (largest tile: 400). Bigger tiles grow faster and hold more.
  • Drag from your tile to a neighbour to send the chosen % of its foglets: an attack on enemy/neutral, a move to your own.
  • Build: tap your tile to open its build wheel (tiles without a structure). Tap a building to build it with that tile's foglets.
  • Zoom: pinch (or mouse wheel) to zoom in, drag empty ground to pan.
  • 🔁 Routes: sending to your own neighbour also sets a route. Every second the source tile forwards all foglets above its reserve (100% − send %, of its cap) to the target. Chain routes to pipe troops from the back to the front. Tap a tile and press ✕ to end its route.
  • Fight: attackers stop at the border and fight for 1–3 seconds. Attack = foglets × attack bonus, defense = defenders × defense bonus. Higher number wins; the winner keeps the difference.
  • Lakes and mountains can't be occupied or crossed.
  • Terrain: 🏔️ Highland (next to mountains) defenders +30% · 🌲 Forest defenders +15% · 🌊 River +25% growth · 💧 Lakeside +15% growth.
  • ✨ Relic tiles: a few neutral tiles hold a shrine with a perk (shown above the number) and a stronger garrison. Whoever holds the tile gets the perk; lose the tile, lose the perk.
  • Neutral (grey) tiles start with a garrison that matches their size and slowly regrow back to it (0.2% of cap per second) if you only damage them. Bots fight each other too: let them weaken each other.
  • Each segment can hold one building, paid with its own foglets. Captured buildings switch sides.

    🧭 Conquest (roguelite): 5 stages from a valley to the whole world. Win a stage and everything you hold collapses into one capital on a bigger map: all your people come along (the capital fills up, the rest waits in a 🏕️ reserve), and each building type becomes a capital bonus = its effect × the share of your segments that had it (4 farms of 10 segments → +60% × 40% = +24% growth). Nothing can be built in a capital. Bots start with part of your strength and their own capitals. Pick a perk at every zoom-out. Lose once and the run ends.

    🌫️ Idle: endless zoom-out. Every defeated foglet gives 🌫️ Fog, every stage won a bonus, every 👑 boss (stage 10, 20, 30...) 💠 Mist: deeper bosses drop more. Spend them in the ⬆️ upgrade tree at any time: upgrades apply at once and are kept forever, each one opens the next (locked ones hide in the fog), and the advanced ones at the end of each branch cost Mist. Rivals get stronger every stage while the numbers keep climbing (K, M, B...). Land runs dry: a tile raises at most 5x its cap in foglets (the bar under its number), and if nobody marches for a while the fog closes in and the bigger army takes the stage. Stages 5, 15, 25... bring 👤 another commander's army (Fog x2). 🏹 Archers let your tiles shoot enemy neighbours (later rivals have them too). 🤖 Auto-attack makes full tiles attack on their own. The 📯 Fog horn gathers Fog in real time, played or away: collect it from the menu. Lose a stage and the run ends; your Fog, Mist and upgrades stay.

    Game time runs at 80% of real time (all per-second numbers are game seconds).

    Privacy: an anonymous visit counter (GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data). Your progress stays on this device.

    Win: wipe out every enemy faction. Keys: space = pause, 1–4 = send %, Esc = deselect / close / leave, U = idle upgrades, A = auto-attack, F11 = fullscreen (desktop app).
